# Document thumbnail icons

These SVG files are bundled locally so document thumbnails do not depend on
third-party network resources at runtime.

- `adobe-acrobat-reader.svg`: Adobe Acrobat Reader logo, sourced from
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Adobe_Acrobat_Reader_icon_(2020).svg).
- `microsoft-word.svg`: Microsoft Word 2019–2025 logo, sourced from
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Microsoft_Office_Word_(2019%E2%80%932025).svg).
- `image.svg`, `video.svg`, `audio.svg`, `extract.svg`, and
  `file-generic.svg`: sourced from
  [Bootstrap Icons](https://icons.getbootstrap.com/), licensed under the
  [MIT License](https://github.com/twbs/icons/blob/main/LICENSE.md).

Adobe Acrobat Reader and Microsoft Word names and logos are trademarks of
their respective owners and are used only to identify document formats.
